## 2. Summary

- The App does **not** require you to create an account or sign in.  
- **Recipe data** is fetched from the public **TheMealDB** API over the internet.  
- **Your preferences** (such as saved favorites, disliked recipes, allergy selections, servings count, and filter choices) are stored **locally on your device** only—we do not operate our own servers that store your personal recipe lists.  
- The App shows **advertisements** using **Google AdMob**. Google may process data as described in their policies (see below).  

---

## 3. Information the App processes

### 3.1 Data stored on your device (local only)

The App may store the following **on your device** using the app’s private storage (e.g. Godot `user://` data). This data is **not** sent to us by default:

- Saved **favorite** recipes (identifiers and titles as needed for the feature)  
- Recipes you have marked as **disliked**  
- **Dinner preferences**, such as: number of people, cook-time bias, dietary filters, kid-friendly option, meal category selection, and allergy-related selections  

Uninstalling the App typically removes this local data (depending on your device and backup settings).

### 3.2 Network requests

When you use features that load recipes or images, the App contacts:

| Service        | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|
| **TheMealDB** (`themealdb.com`) | Fetches meal metadata, ingredients, instructions, and related recipe information. |
| **Image hosts** (URLs provided by TheMealDB) | Loads recipe thumbnail images when you view a recipe. |

Those servers receive standard technical data involved in any web request (for example, your IP address, user agent, and request timing), as determined by those services’ own infrastructure and policies.

### 3.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses **Google AdMob** to display ads (for example, banner and/or interstitial ads).  

Google may use identifiers such as the **advertising ID** on Android, device information, and interaction data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (where allowed) personalize ads.  

How Google uses data is governed by **Google’s Privacy Policy** and, where applicable, the **Google Advertising Policies**. We encourage you to review:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)  

Depending on your region, you may be able to adjust ad personalization in your **Google account settings** or your device’s **Google Ads / Ads** settings.
